# Brainstorm session 2026-10-06 — Daily Beyond English: new brand, new layout, new drawing style

- **Mode:** `/vp-brainstorm` with UI Direction (prototype in `.viepilot/ui-direction/session-2026-10-06/`)
- **Language:** Vietnamese conversation, English artefacts
- **Trigger (owner, verbatim intent):** the app looks dated and inflexible. Re-lay it out like Canva or
  CapCut, make the Character and Scene libraries clearer, add a sidebar. The drawing style is "still very
  ugly" and the logo is not nice. Rename the channel **Daily Beyond English**, use
  `data/Daily Beyond English Avatar Logo.png`, and draw in the style of `data/channels4_banner.jpg`.

## Reference material read

- **Logo:** vector-style cartoon of a woman with round glasses and long wavy brown hair, a red heart on her
  cheek, a green circle, and "DAILY BEYOND" in white over "ENGLISH" in yellow.
- **Banner:** semi-realistic women with glasses (photo-like), beige/brown angled shapes on white, a navy
  title, "LEARN ENGLISH BY REAL TOPICS", "NEW VIDEOS EVERY WED & SAT" and a red Subscribe button.
- **Observation:** the logo and the banner are **two different drawing styles** (cartoon vector vs.
  semi-realistic). The owner chose the banner's for the story pictures.

## Decisions

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| D57 | The channel and app are **Daily Beyond English** (tagline "Learn English by real topics"). The logo is the owner's avatar file |
| D58 | The picture style becomes **semi-realistic like the banner** (replaces the cel-anime style of 2026-10-04). Characters and scene plates are redone and the owner approves a spike first |
| D59 | The glasses-wearing brown-haired teacher is the **presenter character**; a male character in the same style joins her |
| D60 | The brand palette moves to the logo's **green + yellow + white with a red accent** (replacing the violet→cyan palette approved on 2026-10-06 for the intro/outro) |
| D61 | The layout becomes a **collapsible left sidebar** (Projects · Studio · Libraries · Music · Settings) plus **large image-grid libraries with a right-hand detail panel** (Canva style). Steps 1–7 keep the CapCut-like 3-panel shell |
| D62 | The name applies to the **app and logo, the video intro/outro and Jenny's greeting** ("Welcome to Daily Beyond English Channel!"), and the **YouTube package** prompts |

## Constraints

- Behaviour stays: element ids, roles and flows are kept (the 1400+ tests guard them).
- The intro/outro look from Phase 25 stays (the owner approved the morph); only the logo, the name and the
  palette change.
- The new drawing style must pass the existing shot checks (colour check, extra-person check) or those
  checks are adapted. The prompt budget stays under 77 CLIP tokens.
- A semi-realistic look may need a different SDXL checkpoint. Any download needs the owner's approval, and
  the licence must allow commercial use.
