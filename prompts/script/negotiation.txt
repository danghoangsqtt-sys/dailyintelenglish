Structure: two speakers with different goals negotiate toward an agreement
related to the topic.
Turn pattern: proposal, counter-proposal, compromise, repeat — moving
gradually toward a resolution rather than resolving instantly.
Typical turn length: medium.
Natural fillers to use sparingly: "What if we...", "I could agree to...",
"On one condition...", "Let's meet in the middle.".
End with a clear outcome: either an agreement, a compromise, or an
explicit agreement to continue the conversation later — not an abrupt cutoff.
