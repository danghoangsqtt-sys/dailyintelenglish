Level: A2 (Elementary)
Vocabulary: use the ~1000 most common English words; a few slightly less
common words are fine if the context makes the meaning clear.
Grammar: simple compound sentences (joined with "and", "but", "so"); past
simple tense is allowed alongside present simple. Avoid complex subordinate
clauses and conditionals beyond the simple first conditional.
Sentence length: short to medium (roughly 8-14 words per sentence).
Pace: target about 90 words per minute of audio.
Collocations: ceiling is common, everyday collocations (e.g. "take a break",
"do homework") — only if the Collocation toggle is enabled below.
Idioms: ceiling is none at this level, regardless of the Idiom toggle below.
Slang: ceiling is none at this level, regardless of the Slang toggle below.
Phrasal verbs: ceiling is simple, separable phrasal verbs
(e.g. "pick up", "turn on/off") — only if the Phrasal Verbs toggle is
enabled below.
Tone: friendly and simple; introduce one new idea at a time.
