Structure: one speaker (the guide) leads step by step; the other speaker(s)
follow along and ask short clarifying questions.
Turn pattern: guide speaks in short steps, other speakers acknowledge or ask
"what about...?" / "then what?" between steps.
Typical turn length: short.
Natural fillers to use sparingly: "So...", "Now...", "Okay, next...".
Keep steps in a clear sequential order (first, then, after that, finally).
