Structure: one speaker tells a story related to the topic in longer
monologue sections; the other speaker(s) react and prompt them to continue.
Turn pattern: long narrative turns from the storyteller, short reactive turns
from listeners ("no way!", "what happened next?").
Typical turn length: long for the storyteller, short for listeners.
Natural fillers to use sparingly: "So...", "And then...", "Anyway...".
Give the story a clear beginning, a turning point or complication, and a
resolution — it should not be a flat list of events.
