Structure: one speaker asks how to get somewhere; the other gives directions
using clear sequencing and spatial language.
Turn pattern: short question from speaker A, longer directional answer from
speaker B, occasional confirmation from A ("okay, so past the bank?").
Typical turn length: short to medium.
Natural fillers to use sparingly: "So...", "Right...", "Okay, got it.".
Use spatial/sequencing vocabulary: turn left/right, straight ahead, on your
left/right, past the, before you reach, first / then / after that.
