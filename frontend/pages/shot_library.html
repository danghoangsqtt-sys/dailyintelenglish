Shot Library

Ready-made pictures of Alex and Lina in their scenes. Look at each picture once and approve it: a new episode then reuses approved pictures that match its scene, framing, action and mood, and draws only what is missing. A picture of a character that was changed afterwards shows as stale and is never reused.

Add your own pictures

Copy pictures of the two characters into this folder, named scene__framing, then press Import:

  • cafe__duo-wide.png, cafe__duo-close.png: the woman on the left, the man on the right; add -rev (cafe__duo-wide-rev.png) when the man is on the left, or name them left to right: cafe__duo-wide-alex-lina.png.
  • cafe__single-lina.png, cafe__single-alex.png: one person (the character's name).
  • Optional parts after the framing: an action and one of calm, smile, laugh, surprised, thinking, worried, serious, for example cafe__duo-wide__drinking-coffee__smile.png.
  • The scene is a scene id or name without spaces: cafe, city-street, living-room. Pictures should be 16:9; others are cropped to it (the top is kept). Imported pictures wait for your review below.

    Scene backgrounds (pictures without people): put cafe.png and its prompt cafe.txt in the assets and prompts folders of , named by scene key (docs/operations/scene-list-55.md). A background replaces the picture of that scene.

      Talking sprites (the "Podcast: talking characters" video): put transparent 1280 x 1536 PNGs named alex__calm__closed.png, alex__smile__open.png, alex__blink.png, alex__gesture-wave.png ... in (docs/operations/sprite-asset-spec.md). Every picture is checked against the calm, closed-mouth one; a picture that would make the character jump is refused.
