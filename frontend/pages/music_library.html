Music Library

Upload free background tracks, give each one a mood and its licence, and keep the library tidy. The mood and length are used to pick music for each episode automatically.

Where to find free music safely
  • YouTube Audio Library (YouTube Studio → Audio Library): safe for YouTube; filter “Attribution not required”, or copy the credit it shows.
  • Pixabay Music and Mixkit: free for commercial use, no credit needed. Keep the download page link in case a track is ever claimed.
  • Incompetech (Kevin MacLeod): CC BY 4.0 — a credit is required.
  • Free Music Archive: only CC0 or CC BY tracks; avoid NC (non-commercial) and ND.
