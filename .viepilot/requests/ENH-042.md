## Summary

Owner: "Nội dung video còn thiếu phần chào khán giả, cảm ơn đã xem và theo dõi, và giới thiệu sơ bộ về chủ đề hôm nay. nếu nhân vật có thể cười vui càng tốt".

Today the script prompts only allow "a brief intro" in the first section and "a short spoken sign-off" in the last, so the AI often skips the greeting, the topic preview and the thanks.

## Decision (applies to every video type: conversation, Solo presentation, VOX)
- **Opening** (first section, first lines): greet the viewers and welcome them to Daily Beyond English; the speakers say who they are (conversation) or the presenter/narrator introduces themself; one or two sentences preview today's topic and what the viewer will learn.
- **Closing** (last section, last lines): thank the viewers for watching, invite them to like, subscribe and comment, and say goodbye / see you next time.
- Both stay short (they count in the word budget) and in the level's English.
- **Smiles:** conversation storyboard beats covering the opening and the closing use the `smile` (or `laugh`) expression; a Solo presenter waves (`wave` gesture) at the greeting and at the goodbye. A VOX video has nobody on screen (its first and last cards show the welcome and the thanks).
- Word-for-word dialogues (Phase 57) are never changed.
