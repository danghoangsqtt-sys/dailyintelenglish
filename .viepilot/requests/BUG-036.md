# BUG: Intro title breaks "Ep. 2" across two lines

## Meta
- **ID**: BUG-036
- **Type**: Bug
- **Status**: planned
- **Priority**: medium
- **Created**: 2026-10-14
- **Reporter**: owner
- **Source**: wide intro of "The Dinner Party Challenge · Ep. 2: Shopping at the Market"

## Summary
The intro card prints the project name (`<series> · Ep. n: <title>`, `channel_service.py:296`) as one free-wrapping
string, so the browser wrapped between "Ep." and "2:" ("… Challenge · Ep." / "2: Shopping at the Market").

## Plan
- `renderer/src/brand.ts`-level helper `titleParts(title)` in `Brand.tsx`: splits `<series> · Ep. n: <name>` into
  `{series, episode: "Ep. n", name}`; any other title returns `{name: title}`.
- Wide and vertical intros render a series title as two lines: the series name, then "Ep. n: <name>" where
  "Ep.&nbsp;n:" never breaks (`white-space: nowrap`). Plain titles render as before.
- Vitest cases for the split (series title, plain title, title containing "·" without an episode).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] "Ep." and its number always stay on one line in both intros.
- [ ] Series name and episode line appear as separate lines; non-series titles unchanged.
- [ ] Renderer tests pass; a still of the wide intro for Ep. 2 is inspected.
