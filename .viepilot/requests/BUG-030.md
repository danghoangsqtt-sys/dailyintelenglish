# BUG: Re-mixed audio from an old TTS cache silently loses word timings (no karaoke captions)

## Meta

- **Priority**: high
- **Created**: 2026-10-12
- **Reporter**: owner (Gate B-22 review: "không có hiệu ứng karaoke phụ đề"), root cause traced by PM
- **Assignee**: AI
- **Source**: Gate B-22 FAIL, decision D77

## Summary
Karaoke captions work in every mode, but they need per-word timings (`line.words`). The Demo Episode's
`audio_jobs.word_timestamps_json` has `words: []` for all 30 lines. Its per-line cache in
`data/tts_cache/b330d37f-.../` dates from 2026-09-15, before Task 19.2 (2026-09-28) added the `.words.json` sidecar; the
2026-10-06 re-mix reused those MP3s and found no sidecar, so every line fell back to the plain caption (D19.3-c) with no
warning. Projects synthesized after 2026-09-28 have words (656–1260 per project).

## Decision (D77, 2026-10-12)
A cached line without its `.words.json` sidecar counts as a cache miss: re-synthesize it (Edge TTS with
`boundary="WordBoundary"`) before mixing. Then regenerate the Demo Episode audio and video.

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Cache reuse requires both the MP3 and its `.words.json`; a missing sidecar triggers re-synthesis of that line.
- [ ] The mix (or Step 4/5) reports when a finished mix still has lines without word timings, instead of failing silently.
- [ ] Tests: old-cache line (MP3, no sidecar) is re-synthesized; a line with both files is reused.
- [ ] Demo Episode: regenerated audio has word timings on all lines; the re-render shows karaoke captions.
