## Summary

Owner: "nâng cấp chỉnh sửa thư viện nhân vật, thư viện music và setting, thêm lựa chọn ngôn ngữ phần mềm".

## Owner decisions (2026-10-22)
1. **App language:** English and Tiếng Việt, **English by default**; chosen in Settings (and a quick switch in the top bar). Every interface text is translated (sidebar, Dashboard, Channel, Steps 1–7, the libraries, Settings, common messages). Video content (scripts, vocabulary, names, music titles, voices) is never translated.
2. **Character Library:** character list on the left (picture, name, readiness); on the right a fixed profile header (picture, name, gender, accent, voice, readiness, Duplicate/Archive/Delete) and tabs: Conversation pictures (29) · Presenter pictures (21) · Voice · Details. The Scenes tab stays at the top. No feature removed.
3. **Music Library:** the upload box becomes one "Upload music" button (drag-and-drop still works on the page); a compact list (title, mood, pace, length, licence, uses) with search and mood chips; one shared player bar at the bottom; "Edit" opens a side panel; tracks without a licence are flagged.
4. **Settings:** a left menu with sections General (language, light/dark theme, build) · AI (mode, provider order, status) · AI providers (OpenRouter, Gemini, OpenCode Zen as compact cards with a status dot: key saved / connection tested / not set up). Key handling is unchanged.
