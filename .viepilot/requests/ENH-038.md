Facts checked before planning:

## Owner decisions (2026-10-21)

1. **Delete all channel plans, keep the video projects.** Done 2026-10-21 after a backup
   (`data/backups/app-before-channel-reset-20261021.db`, SQLite backup API): 6 topics, 21 series, 203 episodes deleted;
   all 15 projects kept (the 3 Dinner Party projects are no longer linked to an episode).
2. **Dashboard:** a "Start here" box with three choices — continue the latest unfinished project at its next step,
   the next planned episode from the channel plan, a new video from your own topic — plus the 7-step path. All
   existing Dashboard sections stay below.
3. **Steps 1–7:** a "next step" bar on every step: whether this step is done, what is missing, and a button to the next
   step. No feature removed.
4. **Activity Library:** rename in the sidebar; two tabs (Activity pictures | Scene backgrounds); shelves by owner
   (All, Generic, each character, with counts); review-state chips; "Add pictures" always at the top; the advanced
   folder import moved to the end.

The Channel page is redesigned as a three-step flow (Plan → Topics & series → Publishing dates) with a "what to do
next" line; same features, fewer fields shown at once.
