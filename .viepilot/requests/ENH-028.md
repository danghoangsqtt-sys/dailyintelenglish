## Summary
In `podcast_sprites` videos an insert beat's activity picture currently fills the whole frame (Task 32.6d: cover crop,
max 6 s, 0.3 s fades) and hides Alex and Lina. The owner wants the illustration **behind** both characters: it replaces
the scene background for the beat while both sprites keep standing, talking, blinking and gesturing in front.

## Owner decision (D75, 2026-10-12)
- Both characters stay visible in front for the whole beat, **including when the picture shows the speaking character**
  (for example Alex working out behind Alex talking). This is intentional (artistic and teaching choice): no hiding,
  no de-duplication.
- Captions, speaker chips, vocabulary cards and chapter overlays stay on top as today.

## Acceptance Criteria

- [ ] Both sprites remain visible and animated through the beat; no sprite is hidden because of the picture's content.
- [ ] Fade between scene background and activity background keeps the 0.3 s blend; the 6 s cap is re-evaluated in the plan
      (owner choice "background" removes the reason for a short cap).
