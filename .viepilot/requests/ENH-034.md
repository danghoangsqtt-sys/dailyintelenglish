# ENH: Native portrait (9:16) thumbnail pictures

## Meta

## Summary
Every 9:16 cover is cut or carded from a 16:9 picture. The owner finds a landscape picture enlarged into a portrait
cover unreasonable and chose (2026-10-14) **a real portrait picture per kind**, accepting one extra GPU draw for the
AI scene. BUG-035's "no extra GPU draw" constraint is lifted by this decision.

## Plan
1. **Full-bleed portrait layout** (`thumbnail_compose._render_portrait`): when a native portrait picture exists it
   fills 720x1280; badges + logo at the top of the safe area (as now); headline + supporting pill in a text band
   chosen to avoid the faces -- the lower band (y 620-880, left of the button column) by default, the upper band
   (y 240-470) when a head falls in the lower band. A soft gradient from that band's edge gives contrast.
   Without a portrait picture the BUG-035 photo card stays as the fallback.
2. **Composed**: background illustration cropped to portrait (it is a scene, not people) + the two characters drawn
   big, centred, bottom-anchored with their heads in the safe area; headline in the upper band.
3. **Outside AI**: optional second upload `outside_source_9x16.png` (min 360x640, must be taller than wide) and a
   portrait prompt (people in the upper-middle, the lower third plain background for the title). The prompt endpoint
   returns `prompt_9x16`; Step 6 shows a second textarea/copy button and a "⬆ Upload 9:16 picture" button.
   `list_templates` keeps `outside_ai` available when either picture exists; 16:9 without a wide picture raises.
4. **AI scene**: `draw_scenes` asks Qwen for a portrait draw (720x1280) beside each wide draw, with a portrait prompt
   (`recipes.qwen_thumbnail_scene_prompt(..., portrait=True)`), face-detected; stored as `scene["portrait"] =
   {image, faces, heads}`. Old scenes without it fall back to the card. Redraw draws both.
5. Tests: layout picks the band away from heads; composed/outside/qwen portrait use the native picture when present
   and the card otherwise; upload validation; prompt text; draw_scenes item list (engine faked).

## Acceptance Criteria
- [ ] Each kind renders a full-bleed portrait cover from a portrait picture when one exists.
- [ ] Text, badges and logo stay inside the 9:16 safe area and off the faces.
- [ ] Fallback to the BUG-035 card when no portrait picture exists; 16:9 output unchanged.
- [ ] Thumbnail tests pass; a real preview per kind is inspected (owner gate).
