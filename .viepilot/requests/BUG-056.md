## Summary

For the 3-minute Lantern project, narration ran 150.864 s and the export ran 159.8 s. For the 8-minute Apollo 11 project, narration ran 424.176 s and the export ran 432.633 s. The B1 word budget assumes 125 words per minute. Both recordings ran faster than that estimate. Step 5 now shows the actual narration duration before illustration.
