## Summary
Three confirmed inaccuracies in `.viepilot/ARCHITECTURE.md`:

1. **Line 11**: the System Overview text diagram still says
   `↕ REST API / WebSocket (streaming)`. The real implementation is pure
   synchronous polling everywhere — confirmed via `app/api/audio.py`'s own header
   docstring, which explicitly documents `GET /status` as "a plain polling GET, not
   real Server-Sent Events... a deliberate, documented deviation," and no WebSocket
   route exists anywhere in `app/`.
2. **Around line 124-133**: the Mermaid data-flow diagram's Step 4/Step 5 subgraphs
   show `E2[OmniVoice GPU\ngenerate per line]` and `F3[Lips-sync Avatar\nLivePortrait]`
   as active pipeline steps. In reality, Edge TTS is the sole official TTS engine
   (OmniVoice GPU inference was explicitly decided against, per
   `.viepilot/HANDOFF.json`'s `tech_stack.tts_primary` note and
   `.viepilot/TRACKER.md`'s Decision Log), and LivePortrait lip-sync was never
   implemented (only avatar image upload exists — Sub-task 1.7c). Both remain
   correctly logged as deferred in TRACKER.md's Known Issues, but ARCHITECTURE.md's
   own diagram still shows them as if shipped and active.
3. **The embedded `system-overview` Mermaid diagram vs. its sidecar file**
   (`.viepilot/architecture/system-overview.mermaid`): PM diffed them directly and
   confirmed exactly 2 diverging edges — the sidecar includes `LCS`
   (LearningContentService) in both the router fan-out edge
   (`AR --> SS & LCS & TS & AS & VS & THS & PS & YTS`) and the Gemini-dependency edge
   (`SS & LCS --> GEM`); the embedded version in ARCHITECTURE.md is missing `LCS`
   from both.
