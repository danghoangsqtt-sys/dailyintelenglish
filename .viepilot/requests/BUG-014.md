## Summary
`.viepilot/phases/02-testing-polish/tasks/task-2.1b.md`, `task-2.1c.md`, `task-2.5.md`,
and `task-2.6.md` each still carry `**Status**: in_progress` in their Meta section,
even though each one has its own `## PM Acceptance` section explicitly confirming

reading the state files people actually consult, but the individual task cards
themselves are internally inconsistent (a `Status:` field contradicted by that same
file's own `PM Acceptance` section a few dozen lines below it).

card across Phases 1, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, and 8 correctly shows `Status: done` in its Meta
section.

Note: `task-2.1a.md`'s `code_complete_pending_real_run` status was checked too and is
**not** drift — its own PM Acceptance section explicitly explains this is an
intentional, distinct status (the code was accepted, but the real 18-call campaign
outcome was deliberately handed off to `task-2.1b.md` to complete, per a documented
`/vp-auto` control-point decision). Only the 4 files above are genuine oversights.
