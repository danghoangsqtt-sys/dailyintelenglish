## Summary

Owner: "To make video creation take the least time and be as accurate as possible, create one more library with the two
people already talking, in many angles, scenes and positions, laughing and opening the mouth, expressions, flexible
hand and body gestures, so the content looks real. Later the AI video creation can put in these ready shots to be as
fast as possible, without waiting to generate from scratch: only the scenes that are not in the library are made."

Idea: a pre-generated, tagged **shot library** (characters x scene x framing x expression x gesture) that the storyboard
matches first; only missing combinations are generated, and every generated shot can be added back to the library.
Design and tasks: `docs/implementation/phase-29-shot-library.md`.
