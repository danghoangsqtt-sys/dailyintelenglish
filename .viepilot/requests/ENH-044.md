## Owner decisions (2026-10-22, brainstorm `docs/brainstorm/session-2026-10-22-viral-shorts.md` §2b)
1. **Do everything, then wait for review**: script → learning → voice audio → visual plan (a Solo plan is approved automatically only when it has no issue and no fact-review flag) → render → thumbnail → YouTube package. An episode with an error or a fact warning stops at that step and says why.
2. **No automatic YouTube upload**; the owner publishes.
3. **A night window + N days ahead** (e.g. 23:00–06:00, episodes whose publishing date is within the next N days); the computer is kept awake inside the window.
4. **The app opens with Windows** (at sign-in) and waits for the window.
5. **AI from Settings** (cloud or local) with **at most N videos per night**.
6. **Source**: the Channel plan episodes with publishing dates (Shorts schedules come with the viral Shorts phases).
7. **Report in the app** (what was made, what waits for review, what failed and why) + a Windows notification.
8. Done **before** the viral Shorts.
