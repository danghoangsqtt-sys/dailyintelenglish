## Summary

Uploading Lina's `gesture-count-1` / `gesture-count-2` front pictures returns HTTP 422 every time while `talk`, `point-left/right` and `emphasize` pass. `sprite_service.measure` (gesture mode) refuses any picture whose top 20% of the figure (`HEAD_FRACTION = 0.20`, head plus neck and shoulder tops) differs from `calm__closed` by more than 4% (`SILHOUETTE_LIMIT`), and also checks the head's horizontal centre (`HEAD_SIDE_LIMIT = 10 px`). The count prompts (`presenter_asset_plan.py:29-31`) ask for fingers "near the shoulder", which puts the hand in that zone. Measured on Lina's approved base: a 70×130 px hand 40 px into the zone passes (3.3%); 90 px fails (10.3%, "head moved sideways +14px"). No profile has ever approved `count-1/2/3`, `think` or `wave`, so the 21-picture pack (Phase 49.10 / V49) cannot be completed.

Second defect: the refusal text goes to `#profile-message` at the top of the profile panel (`character_profiles.js` `notice()`, `characters.html:151`) and the card returns to "Empty", so the owner sees only a console 422.

## Acceptance criteria
- The head check compares the head itself (e.g. the reference head/face mask or colour inside the reference head silhouette), so a hand beside or in front of the shoulder/face does not count as a changed head; identity drift in the face is still refused.
- Or, if the rule stays, the count/think/wave prompts state where the hand may go and the card shows that limit.
- A failed upload shows its reason on the card itself (and keeps the error visible), not only at the top of the panel.
- Regression tests: raised-hand gesture passes; redrawn head still fails; browser test shows the reason on the card.
- Real check: Lina's count-1 picture uploads and can be approved.
