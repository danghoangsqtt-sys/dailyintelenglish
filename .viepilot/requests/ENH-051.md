## Summary

Owner: "nâng cấp giao diện của Trang BẢNG ĐIỀU KHIỂN và KẾ HOẠCH KÊNH và CÀI ĐẶT" — after the Automation studio (Phase 67 Amendment A), the three other main pages get the same professional studio look, so the app reads as one product instead of pages of text.

## Scope

- **Dashboard:** the dark studio bar (title, the numbers of the channel, actions), "Start here" as three large action cards, the 7 steps as a step rail, "Next to publish" and "Today" side by side, the roadmap as a progress rail, the production board as coloured columns with counted headers, the next two weeks as a calendar strip of day columns, and the project grid under a studio card header with its filters and search.
- **Channel plan:** the studio bar with the counts as tiles, the 3 steps as a progress rail, each topic as a card with a coloured header and its series as cards with a mode badge, a tidier series control row, and the publishing calendar grouped by week as day chips.
- **Settings:** the studio bar, a left menu of large tiles (icon, name, one-line description), each section's cards with icon headers, and the AI providers as status tiles.

## Kept

Every element id, data attribute and API the scripts and tests use; every feature and control; light/dark themes; the Vietnamese interface. No backend change.
