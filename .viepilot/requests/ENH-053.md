## Summary

Owner: "lập trình và đóng gói 1 phiên bản cho máy tính cấu hình yếu như laptop của tôi để tránh cho người dùng cảm giác dư tính năng" — on a machine that cannot draw pictures with local AI (the owner's laptop: 24 GB RAM, 4 GB VRAM), the buttons for those features should not be shown.

Owner choices (2026-10-22): **auto-detection plus a Lite package** — one source, two packages (Full and Lite); Lite hides the local-GPU picture features by default, Full hides them when the machine has no capable GPU, and a Settings switch changes it. Hide **only** the four local AI picture features: drawing a scene picture, drawing missing activity illustrations (Step 5), the topic picture (Step 6), the AI scene thumbnail (Step 6). Local text AI (Ollama) stays.

## Kept

Nothing is removed from the code; a hidden feature comes back with the switch. Pictures already in the library are used as before on every machine.
