## Owner decisions (2026-10-22)
1. **Characters (conversation and the Solo presenter): "Sôi nổi, nhiều động tác"** — the speaker leans toward the listener and bobs on stressed words; the listener nods and shifts weight; gestures change per phrase, short lines included; pose changes use a quick crossfade (~0.15 s) while the mouth keeps direct swaps; larger amplitude and a camera push-in on the speaker at emphasis. Still nothing pasted on the pictures and never squashed or distorted.
2. **VOX: all four groups**
   - varied transitions: paper slide, diagonal wipe, zoom-through, page flip, iris circle, whip-pan with motion blur;
   - morph between segments: the title and a decorative shape morph smoothly (like PowerPoint Morph); the map zooms from the world to the region;
   - camera and background motion: push-in / slow pan per segment, parallax paper scraps and shapes, a drifting light;
   - per-element effects: bouncy words, hand-drawn underlines and arrows, chart numbers counting up, a "Subscribe" stamp at the end, map pins dropping in.
