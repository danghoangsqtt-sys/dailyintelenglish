# ENH: Gemini first-pass section length at 0.61× target: every section needs one repair

## Meta
- **ID**: ENH-015
- **Type**: Enhancement
- **Status**: open
- **Priority**: medium
- **Created**: 2026-09-26
- **Reporter**: PM (Gate B-11)

## Summary
With Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite as the primary, the first pass in the full section prompt lands at a
median **0.61×** the section target, so all 5 sections of a B1 job are repaired once (11 calls per
job). A bare single-section probe gave 1.00×, so the full prompt's length instruction is weaker
than it needs to be for this model. Tuning it could roughly halve the calls: faster, and gentler
on the free quota.

## Acceptance Criteria (proposal)
- [ ] An evidence-based prompt change (the thresholds stay pinned) that raises the Gemini
      first-pass median to ≥ 0.85× target, measured on real calls.
- [ ] No regression on local qwen (the fallback) or on OpenRouter models.
