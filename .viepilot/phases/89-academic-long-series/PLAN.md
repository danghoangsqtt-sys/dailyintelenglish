## Tasks

1. **89.1 Long `roots`** — 16:9, 8–12 min: one root with 10–15 words or 2–3 roots on one theme; each root a chapter;
   a practice part (guess a new word's meaning from its parts) and a review; Shorts can be cut from it.
2. **89.2 Long `grammar`** — 16:9, 10–15 min: each scene a chapter, more examples, a practice exercise with countdown,
   the summary table.
3. **89.3 Series** — Channel plan series presets ("Một gốc – cả họ từ", "Tiền tố", "Hậu tố & họ từ", "Ngữ pháp bản
   chất", "Lỗi người Việt hay mắc"); episode number on screen and in the title; the series remembers used roots/points and
   takes the next from the curated list; the quiz of episode N is answered at the start of episode N+1; Automatic videos.
4. **89.4 Verify** — tests; one long video of each kind and a two-episode series run.

Task cards are written when the phase starts.
