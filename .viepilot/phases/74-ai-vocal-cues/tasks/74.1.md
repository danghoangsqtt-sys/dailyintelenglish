# 74.1 — Cue grammar and Edge TTS rendering

Status: verified_pending_owner_audio_review (2026-10-23). Targeted TTS/cue checks are included in 276 passing tests; real voice listening pending.

## Objective

Keep three existing tags working, add `ha-ha`/`hi-hi` laugh variants, and render `[hắng giọng]` as a short “ahem” plus a bounded lower-pitch clause that returns to the pinned voice's original settings. Preserve timing and cache correctness.

## Paths / allowed_files and file-level plan

- `backend/ai/tts/vocal_cues.py`: central parser/formatter/stripper for old and new tags, typed cue parts and bounded post-ahem span; unknown bracket text is never interpreted as a cue.
- `backend/ai/tts/tts_service.py`: synthesize cue and speech spans with the selected voice, safe pitch override for the short post-ahem span, measured decode/join with offsets, and error propagation outside DB locks.
- `backend/core/constants.py`: named cue recipe version, span limit and safe prosody deltas.
- `backend/core/voice.py`: include recipe version only for recognized cue-bearing lines in cache/stale-completion fingerprint; keep ordinary cached lines valid.
- `tests/test_vocal_cues.py`: planned parser/stripper cases including legacy tags, punctuation, variants and unknown tags.
- `tests/test_tts_service.py`: planned fake Edge calls for per-span settings, restoration, cache invalidation and synthesis error.
- `tests/test_tts_word_boundary.py`: planned decoded MP3 duration/offset evidence and no karaoke words for cue audio.

## Risks and acceptance

Avoid global `AudioSegment` settings drift or mutating the persisted speaker dict. MP3 decode/export uses configured ffmpeg in a worker thread. `Haiz` is only a candidate to audition. Result: the same speaker voice is heard throughout, pitch change ends promptly, and old `[cười]` scripts still play. Targeted verification and live listening are planned for execution; no tests run at planning time.
