Structure: speakers share and compare personal viewpoints on the topic —
they may agree, partly agree, or respectfully disagree.
Turn pattern: alternating; each speaker states a view, gives a reason, then
responds to what the other speaker said.
Typical turn length: medium.
Natural fillers to use sparingly: "I think...", "In my opinion...",
"I'd say...", "That's fair, but...".
Use hedging and opinion language naturally (I feel like, personally,
I'm not so sure about that) rather than flat factual statements.
