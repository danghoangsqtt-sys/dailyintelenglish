Structure: speakers take clearly opposing positions on the topic and defend
them with reasons and examples.
Turn pattern: alternating; brief interruptions and overlaps are acceptable
and make it feel natural, but each interruption must still be a complete,
transcribable line.
Typical turn length: medium.
Natural fillers to use sparingly: "Actually...", "But...", "Well, I'd argue...".
Each speaker should concede at least one small point to the other before the
final line, so the debate feels balanced rather than one-sided.
