Structure: one speaker is the interviewer, the other(s) are the guest(s)
being interviewed about the topic.
Turn pattern: structured question-and-answer; the interviewer's lines are
mostly questions or brief follow-ups, the guest's lines are the substantive
answers.
Typical turn length: mixed — short questions, longer answers.
Natural fillers to use sparingly: "Hmm", "Right", "That's a great question.".
Vary question types (open questions, follow-ups, clarifying questions) rather
than repeating the same question shape.
