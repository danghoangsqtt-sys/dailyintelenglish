Structure: one speaker (host) explains facts or concepts about the topic in a
clear, structured way; the other speaker(s) ask clarifying questions or add a
supporting detail.
Turn pattern: host explains a point, co-host reacts or asks a follow-up
question, repeat for each sub-topic.
Typical turn length: medium.
Natural fillers to use sparingly: "Right, so...", "That makes sense.",
"Interesting — and...".
Keep the register accessible and accurate — no invented statistics or facts;
speak in general, verifiable terms about the topic rather than citing specific
numbers unless they are common knowledge.
